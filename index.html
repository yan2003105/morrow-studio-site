05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I receive?

A finished 15–20 second vertical (9:16) film, delivered as MP4 — ready for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and your product page.

Do I need a live shoot?

No. Our films are crafted without filming. Live-action shoots are quoted separately if you want one.

How many revisions are included?

One revision round is included in every product film.

How long does it take?

5 business days from your confirmed brief and required assets.

What do you need from me?

Your confirmed brief and product/brand assets (photos, packaging, logo). We confirm the full list before production starts.

What payment methods do you accept?

PayPal and bank transfer (USD). 50% deposit to start production, 50% on final delivery.

Have a product worth pausing for?

Tell us about your product, your goal and what you need—we’ll shape the scope from there.
